# 1 Library borrowing
Students may borrow up to 5 books for 14 days. Books can be renewed once online if no one else has reserved them.

# 2 IT account password reset
Students can reset their university account password at the IT Helpdesk (room E.005) or online with their registered recovery email.

# 3 Student card replacement
A lost student card can be replaced at Student Affairs (room I.102). The replacement fee is 50,000 VND and the new card is ready in 3 working days.

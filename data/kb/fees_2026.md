# 1 Tuition per credit
The tuition rate for the 2026 academic year is 450,000 VND per credit for regular programs.

# 2 Retake fee
Retake credits are charged at 1.5 times the regular credit rate.

# 3 Payment deadline
Tuition must be paid by the end of week 6. Students who pay late receive a reminder and may have registration suspended after week 8.

# 4 Refunds
Students who withdraw from a course before the end of week 2 receive a full refund for that course. No refund is given after week 2.

# 4.2 Retaking a course
Students may retake a course they failed or want to improve. Retake credits are charged at 1.5 times the normal credit rate. Final-year students must register retakes before week 2 of the semester.

# 5.3 Thesis registration
Students may register a thesis when they have at least 120 accumulated credits and a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0. Registration opens in week 1 and closes in week 3 of the semester.

# 5.4 Late thesis registration
Late thesis registration requires written approval from both the Faculty and the proposed supervisor. Requests must be submitted to the Training Office no later than week 5.

# 6.1 Credit load
A regular semester load is between 14 and 30 credits. Registering more than 30 credits requires approval from the academic advisor.

# 7.2 Exam absence
Students absent from a final exam for medical reasons must submit a medical certificate to the Training Office within 3 working days. Approved students take the exam in the next exam period.

# 8.1 Leave of absence
Students may request a leave of absence for up to two semesters. Tuition already paid for the current semester is not refunded after week 4.

# 8.3 Transfer credits
Credits from another university may be recognised if the course content matches at least 80 percent and the grade is at least 5.0 out of 10.
